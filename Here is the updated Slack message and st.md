I've written a script to make Keka logs easier to read. Since we can't use extensions, you can use this simple trick to instantly see *Work Duration*, *Break Time*, and *Exact Leaving Time* directly on your page.

*Command:*
```javascript:(()=>{fetch("https://gist.githubusercontent.com/Umang-Vadadoriya/ffc09708226db8bef988a0ecf1848518/raw/KekaEnhance.js").then(r=>r.text()).then(eval);})();```

🛠️ *How to Use*

1.  Right-click on your Chrome Bookmarks Bar and select *Add Page...*
2.  *Name:* `Keka Script`
3.  *URL:* Copy & paste the command above.
4.  *Save.*
5.  *To Run:* Just click the *Keka Script* bookmark whenever you open the Keka Attendance page.

*(Note: You will need to click the bookmark or run the command again if you refresh the page.)*
